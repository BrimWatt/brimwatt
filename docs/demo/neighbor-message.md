# Message to a Tesla owner: would you volunteer as a tester?

Send with `wattsync-tesla-pitch.mp4` (46 s, made-up sample numbers; rebuild with `docs/demo-src/make_neighbor_demo.py`).

> Hi [name]! I've been building a small solar-charging controller for my own car charger, and I've got it talking to a Tesla over Bluetooth. I need a second Tesla to try it on before I trust it, and I'm hoping you'd be up for volunteering. This 45 second video shows what it does (the numbers are made up).
>
> What I'd need from you: about 30 minutes, once, in your driveway with the car plugged in. You tap your key card on the console to approve it (the same as adding a phone key), then I change the charging current a few times and watch how the car responds. There's no Tesla account, password or cloud service involved: the box talks to the car directly over Bluetooth. It never wakes the car unless that's allowed, and afterwards I can remove the key, or you can (Controls > Locks).
>
> Honest caveat: it's an early test. So far I've only tried it on one Model Y. Which model and year do you have? It should work with a Model 3, Model Y, or a 2021 or newer Model S or X.
>
> No pressure at all. "No thanks" is a perfectly good answer. If you're up for it, tell me a time that works.
